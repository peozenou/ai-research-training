SYNTHETIC TRAINING MATERIAL

This folder contains a fictional research project created for the
"Using AI to Automate Research Outputs" training session. The country, study,
authors, data, estimates, and conclusions are invented for teaching purposes.

Contents

- paper.pdf
  Synthetic working paper describing the study, methods, results, and policy
  implications.

- outputs/main_results.csv
  Main outcome estimates reported in the paper.

- outputs/figure_main_outcomes.png
  Main outcome comparison used in the paper and slide deck.

- outputs/figure_subgroup_effects.png
  Heterogeneity estimates used in the paper and slide deck.

- antenatal-reminders-needs-improvement.pptx
  A deliberately weak but realistic 14-slide presentation. It contains
  inconsistent formatting, small text, excessive detail, weak titles,
  poorly integrated evidence, and incomplete branding decisions.

The files are internally consistent so an AI agent can use the paper and
outputs to verify and improve the presentation.
